Sweetness with a purpose

Joy Sweet brings the comfort and craft of Cantonese 糖水 to the heart of Manchester’s Chinatown.

Our story

A bowl to soften the day

In Cantonese kitchens, 糖水 are gentle sweet soups and puddings you turn to for comfort: warming when it’s cold, cooling when it’s warm, and always made to be shared.

“解忧食糖” captures that feeling — a little sweetness to ease your worries. At Joy Sweet, familiar favourites meet playful modern combinations, from silky tofu pudding and slow-cooked beans to mango snow mountains and coconut served in the shell.

Made with careThoughtful textures, comforting flavours and carefully prepared toppings.
Hot or icedTraditional warming bowls alongside refreshing snow ices.
Here in ChinatownA cosy stop for dessert, conversation and a quiet pause.
